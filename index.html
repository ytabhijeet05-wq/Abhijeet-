GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

How do I place an order? ＋

Choose a poster, select “Order now,” and submit the order enquiry form. An enquiry is not a confirmed purchase until you receive confirmation from the store.

Can I order a poster if it has no product link? ＋

Yes. The email enquiry form works even when an external product link has not been added.

How do I add my own posters? ＋

Open script.js and edit the PRODUCTS array. Replace each placeholder name, imageUrl, price, category, and optional productUrl.

How do I ask about delivery or returns? ＋

Use the contact details below to ask before ordering. Add your actual delivery, refund, and return policies before publishing.
